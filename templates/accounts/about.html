About the TUK Bursary Portal

Helping Technical University of Kenya students access financial aid for their education.

What is the TUK Bursary Portal?

The TUK Bursary & Scholarship Portal is the official digital platform for managing financial aid applications at Technical University of Kenya. It enables eligible students from financially vulnerable backgrounds to apply for bursaries and scholarships, track their applications, and confirm receipt of disbursed funds — all through a single, secure online system.

Who Can Apply?

Eligibility
Currently enrolled TUK students who are experiencing financial hardship and are unable to meet their fee obligations.
Email Requirement
You must have an active TUK institutional email address ending in @students.tuk.ac.ke to register.
Documents Needed
National ID or Birth Certificate, fee statement, letter from school, and a filled application form.
Frequency
Applications are reviewed each semester. You may apply once per semester.

How It Works

1

Register Your Account

Sign up with your official TUK student email. Your registration is verified against your student number.

2

Download the Application Form

Download the official blank application form from the portal, print and fill it, then scan or photograph it.

3

Submit Your Application

Upload the filled form, supporting documents, and fill in your personal statement about your financial situation.

4

Track Your Application

The Bursary Committee reviews applications and updates statuses: Pending → Qualified → Approved → Disbursed.

5

Receive & Confirm Funds

Once disbursed, collect your cheque from the Bursary Office, deposit it at the bank, and upload your bank deposit slip to the portal.

📞

Contact the Bursary Office

For assistance, visit the Student Affairs Division at the TUK Main Campus or email bursary@tuk.ac.ke.
